Giant Robot

Erik · Professional case study

The royalty side of the business. It lets users create CWR files and royalty-friendly Excel formats of their songs for registering with IPRS, PRS and other societies, then takes the payment reports back in, distributes the money label-wise and creates the invoices.

Stack
Bootstrap · HTML · CSS · JS · CodeIgniter 4

An account of my professional work. Client identities and business-specific details are withheld.

How a royalty flows through Giant Robot

  1. CWR & Excel files
  2. Society registration
  3. Payment reports
  4. Label-wise payout
  5. Invoices

The challenge

Songs have to be registered with royalty societies in the formats they accept. When the societies pay out, those payments have to be split correctly across labels and invoiced.

How it works

From registration to payout.

  1. Create registration files

    Users create CWR (Common Works Registration) files and royalty-friendly Excel formats of their songs, so they are easy to register on IPRS, PRS and other societies.

  2. Upload payment reports

    The admin gets the payment reports from the societies and uploads them into the system.

  3. Distribute label-wise

    The system distributes the collected payments label by label.

  4. Invoices and reports

    For the collected royalties, invoice creation and the rest of the payout work happens in the same system.
